About Maalga Restaurant

A Culinary Journey Through the Mountains of Afghanistan & Valleys of Peshawar

Located in the heart of Johar Town, Lahore at Khayaban-e-Firdousi, Maalga Restaurant brings time-honored tribal recipes straight to your table. From slow-cooked Dumba Roast seasoned with pure rock salt and spices, to fragrant Kabuli Pulao topped with caramelized carrots and raisins, every dish tells a story of heritage and passion.
