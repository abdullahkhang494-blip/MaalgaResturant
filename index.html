About Maalga Restaurant

Authentic Tribal Flavors Preserved For Generational Dining

Located at Khayaban-e-Firdousi in Johar Town, Lahore, Maalga Restaurant brings tribal Afghan and Peshawari culinary tradition directly to your dining table. Cooked slowly over open charcoal fires with organic rock salt, mutton fat, and simple, authentic mountain spices, every platter offers an unmatched gastronomic experience.
